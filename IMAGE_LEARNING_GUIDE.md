# 📸 Image Learning Guide for Students

## How to Use Images with ASSka AI

ASSka AI can analyze images and help you learn! Here's what you can do:

### 🎯 What Can AI Do With Your Images?

#### 1. **Solve Math Problems** 📐
Upload a photo of:
- Math equations
- Geometry problems
- Algebra questions
- Calculus problems

**Example prompts:**
- "Solve this equation step by step"
- "Explain how to solve this problem"
- "What's the answer and show your work"

#### 2. **Explain Diagrams & Charts** 📊
Upload images of:
- Science diagrams
- Flowcharts
- Graphs and charts
- Biology/Chemistry diagrams

**Example prompts:**
- "Explain this diagram"
- "What does this chart show?"
- "Describe the process in this image"

#### 3. **Read & Summarize Text** 📝
Upload photos of:
- Textbook pages
- Notes
- Worksheets
- Study materials

**Example prompts:**
- "Summarize this text"
- "Explain the main points"
- "What are the key concepts here?"

#### 4. **Translate Languages** 🌍
Upload images with:
- Foreign language text
- Signs or menus
- Documents in other languages

**Example prompts:**
- "Translate this to English"
- "What does this say?"

#### 5. **Identify & Explain** 🔍
Upload images of:
- Historical artifacts
- Scientific specimens
- Art pieces
- Unknown objects

**Example prompts:**
- "What is this?"
- "Explain what I'm looking at"
- "Tell me about this image"

### 📱 How to Upload Images

1. **Click the attachment icon** (📎) in the chat input box
2. **Select your image** from your device
3. **Wait for upload** - you'll see "Uploading image... X%"
4. **Image appears** with helpful suggestions
5. **Type your question** about the image
6. **Press Enter** to get AI's response

### ✨ Tips for Best Results

✅ **DO:**
- Use clear, well-lit photos
- Make sure text is readable
- Keep images under 5MB
- Ask specific questions
- Be clear about what you want to know

❌ **DON'T:**
- Upload blurry images
- Use images with poor lighting
- Upload very large files (>5MB)
- Ask vague questions

### 🎓 Study Use Cases

#### For Homework Help:
1. Upload problem → "Explain how to solve this"
2. Upload diagram → "What does each part do?"
3. Upload notes → "Quiz me on this material"

#### For Test Prep:
1. Upload practice problems → "Check my work"
2. Upload study guide → "Summarize key points"
3. Upload formulas → "When do I use this?"

#### For Learning New Topics:
1. Upload textbook page → "Explain this concept simply"
2. Upload complex diagram → "Break this down for me"
3. Upload example → "Show me similar problems"

### 🚀 Example Workflow

**Scenario: Math Homework**
1. Take photo of math problem
2. Upload to ASSka AI
3. Ask: "Solve this step by step and explain each step"
4. AI shows solution with explanations
5. Ask follow-up: "Can you show another example?"
6. Learn the concept!

**Scenario: Science Study**
1. Take photo of biology diagram
2. Upload to ASSka AI
3. Ask: "Label and explain each part"
4. AI identifies and explains components
5. Ask: "Quiz me on this diagram"
6. Test your knowledge!

### 💡 Pro Tips

- **Combine text and images**: Upload an image and ask specific questions
- **Follow-up questions**: After AI analyzes, ask for more details
- **Request examples**: Ask AI to create similar problems
- **Check your work**: Upload your solution and ask AI to verify
- **Learn step-by-step**: Request detailed explanations

Use images to:
- ✅ Understand difficult concepts visually
- ✅ Get instant homework help
- ✅ Practice problem-solving
- ✅ Prepare for exams
- ✅ Learn at your own pace
- ✅ Get explanations in simple terms
