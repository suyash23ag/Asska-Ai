## Tips for Best Results

### ✅ DO:
- **Be specific**: "A red apple on a wooden table" vs "an apple"
- **Add details**: Include colors, style, mood, setting
- **Describe composition**: "centered", "close-up", "wide angle"
- **Mention style**: "realistic", "cartoon", "watercolor", "digital art"
- **Set the scene**: Include background, lighting, atmosphere

### ❌ DON'T:
- Be too vague: "something cool"
- Request inappropriate content
- Expect exact replicas of copyrighted characters
- Use overly complex descriptions (keep it clear)

## Limitations

### Cannot Generate:
- ❌ Real people's faces (celebrities, public figures)
- ❌ Copyrighted characters (exact replicas)
- ❌ Inappropriate or harmful content
- ❌ Text-heavy images (text may be unclear)
- ❌ Very specific logos or brands

### May Have Issues With:
- ⚠️ Very complex scenes with many elements
- ⚠️ Precise text rendering
- ⚠️ Exact measurements or technical accuracy
- ⚠️ Multiple specific people in one image

## Troubleshooting

### Image Not Generating?
1. Check your prompt includes trigger words
2. Make sure prompt is clear and appropriate
3. Try simplifying your description
4. Refresh and try again

### Not What You Expected?
1. Add more specific details
2. Describe the style you want
3. Mention colors, mood, composition
4. Try different wording

### Want Variations?
1. Ask: "Generate another version of..."
2. Modify your prompt slightly
3. Add or change details
4. Request different style or angle
